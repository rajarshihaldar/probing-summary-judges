Evaluation Criteria:
Fluency (1-5) - the quality of individual sentences. Sentences in the summary
"should have no formatting problems, capitalization errors or obviously
ungrammatical sentences (e.g., fragments, missing components) that make the
text difficult to read."

Rubric:
- 5: All sentences are well-formed and easy to read. No grammatical errors,
     formatting problems, or unnatural phrasing of any kind.
- 4: Nearly all sentences are fluent. At most one minor grammatical issue or
     slightly awkward phrasing that does not impede readability.
- 3: Some sentences are fluent but there are noticeable grammatical errors,
     fragments, or unnatural constructions in one or two sentences.
- 2: Multiple sentences contain clear grammatical errors or formatting problems
     that make parts of the summary difficult to read.
- 1: The majority of sentences are poorly formed, fragmented, or so
     ungrammatical that the summary is hard to understand.

Evaluation Steps:
1. Read each sentence of the summary individually.
2. Check for grammatical errors, capitalization issues, formatting problems,
   fragments, or missing sentence components.
3. Check for unnatural phrasing that impedes readability.
4. Note how many sentences are affected and the severity of errors.
5. Based on your analysis and the rubric above, assign a score from 1 to 5.
